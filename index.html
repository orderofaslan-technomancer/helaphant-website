THE HELAPHANT

What the hell
is a Helaphant?

A fusion of two ancient forces.

Hel — Norse goddess, half living, half dead; neither a cruel demon nor a merciful savior, but the unemotional prison guard of those who failed a valiant death.

The Elephant — matriarch, harmony, and devoted mother; modeling nurturing leadership and guardian of memory.

The Helaphant is the woman who holds the savage and the sacred in the same hands.

She creates and protects with the same intensity.

— Waging war through art.
